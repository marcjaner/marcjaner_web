(e.g. `reading-notebook.md` → `/projects/reading-notebook`). Update `started` to the
real date, and replace everything below the frontmatter — including these instructions
— with your own prose before setting `draft: false`, otherwise this boilerplate gets
published.

Fill `technologies` with the real stack — each entry renders as a chip on the project
card and the overview. Add `liveUrl` or `githubUrl` to get Live and Source links, and
`featuredImage` to supply the project's artwork; all of them are optional, so leave them
out until you have real values rather than filling in placeholders.
