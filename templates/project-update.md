Copy this file into `src/content/project-updates/` with a filename that becomes the URL
slug (e.g. `choosing-a-small-first-version.md`), and set `project` to the filename of
the project it belongs to, without the `.md`. Update `date` to the real date, and
replace everything below the frontmatter — including these instructions — with your
own prose before setting `draft: false`, otherwise this boilerplate gets published.
Set `showInBlog: true` if the entry should also appear in Writing and its tag archives.

A journal entry is a reflective essay, not a changelog. Write about the thinking behind
a stretch of work rather than listing what shipped.

Some prompts that usually help:

- What decision did you actually make since the last entry, and what pushed it?
- What did you try that did not work, and what did that teach you?
- What are you deliberately not doing yet, and why?
- What do you want to try next, and what would make you change your mind?

Write in prose, as long or as short as it needs to be. Delete these prompts once you
start writing.
